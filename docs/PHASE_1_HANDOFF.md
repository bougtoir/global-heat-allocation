## Principal scope risk

The journal explicitly requires applied energy focus. A result dominated by
moving heat to cold, remote, or unpopulated cells would face a high desk-reject
risk. Storage, transport, capacity, and conversion constraints must therefore
be central to the analysis rather than appended as discussion.
