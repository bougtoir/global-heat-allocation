# JCP final traceability, fabrication, and consistency audit

**Passed, with explicit evidence limits retained.**

The manuscript's numerical claims are traceable to frozen source artifacts, no
missing engineering or environmental parameter was replaced by an invented
value, and the theoretical and practical branches retain separate units. The
audit does not convert the six conditional modes into supported practical
endpoints.

## Claim-evidence controls

The claim matrix contains 11 controlled claims:

- 5 Level 1 directly supported claims;
- 2 Level 2 interpretive but defensible claims;
- 4 Level 3 unsupported or prohibited claims.

All listed support-artifact paths exist. The two Level 3 rows that intentionally
record `none` as support are prohibitions against declaring a safe sink or a
deployable relocation scheme. Their absence of supporting evidence is therefore
the control, not a missing audit item.
