# Applied Energy requirements snapshot

Accessed and rechecked 2026-09-24 UTC from the official
Elsevier/ScienceDirect pages.

## Scope and article type

The selected article type is **Full Length Research Article**, defined by the
official guide as original, high-quality research presenting novel scientific
findings.

The live scope states that submissions should bridge research, development,
and implementation and that the focus must be on applied aspects of energy.
This makes an auditable real thermal-energy case, calibrated technology,
finite operation, and engineering/environmental constraints essential to the
proposed two-scale paper. Additional theoretical optimization alone would not
close the journal-fit gate.

The study fits most directly under the journal's explicit interests in:

- optimal use of energy resources;
- analysis and optimization of energy processes;
- energy storage;
- decarbonization of heat;
- optimization and decision-making methods for energy-system problems;
- data analytics for energy problems; and
- environmental consequences of energy use.

The official scope requires focus on applied aspects of energy. A purely
geographical redistribution exercise would therefore be insufficient. The
manuscript must connect the theoretical bound to thermal-energy transport,
storage, capacity, conversion losses, and operationally interpretable
constraints without implying that planetary-scale relocation is presently
feasible.

## Current publication model

The journal supports both:

- subscription publication with no publication fee charged to authors; and
- optional open access, for which the journal page listed an article publishing
  charge of USD 4,210 excluding taxes at the access date.

The author must choose the publication route during submission.

## Required submission components tracked by this project

The official guide lists requirements or dedicated guidance for:

- title page;
- abstract and keywords;
- highlights;
- graphical abstract;
- mathematical formulae;
- tables and separate figure/artwork files;
- supplementary material;
- research-data statement and data linking;
- competing-interests and funding declarations;
- generative-AI declaration when applicable; and
- a submission checklist.

The retrieved official guide did not expose a stable explicit manuscript
word/page cap in the text snapshot used for this audit. The final QC must
re-read the live guide immediately before submission rather than rely on an
unofficial secondary limit.

The live guide identifies a single-anonymized review process and initial
editorial suitability screening. Full-length research articles must present
novel scientific findings.

## AI-related guidance

Elsevier's current policy permits responsible AI assistance with human
oversight but requires authors to verify accuracy and sources, edit the output,
remain accountable, and disclose AI use in a separate declaration. AI tools
must not be listed as authors. AI-generated or AI-altered figures are subject
to separate restrictions; this project will generate scientific figures
directly from analysis code.
