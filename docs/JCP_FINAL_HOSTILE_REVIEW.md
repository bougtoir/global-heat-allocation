# JCP final hostile review

Date: 2026-09-25 UTC

## Scope and verdict

This review attacks the final manuscript as a Journal of Cleaner Production
handling editor and as reviewers in cleaner production/industrial ecology,
environmental systems/LCA, thermal energy, optimization, and
reproducibility/statistics. It reviews the frozen analysis, compressed
manuscript, tables, figures, supplement, highlights, graphical abstract, and
cover letter.

**Verdict: WEAK GO after author-controlled metadata are completed.** The
conservation-aware and evidence-gated method is defensible and relevant to JCP,
but desk-rejection risk remains material because the global analysis is a
reduced-order screen, the applied branch has zero evidence-supported endpoints,
and a case-specific LCA cannot be completed from the available evidence.

## Reviewer bottom lines

### JCP handling editor

The paper fits JCP as a boundary-control and problem-shifting detection method,
not as a climate-engineering or infrastructure proposal. The negative applied
endpoint and missing LCA remain visible editorial risks but are not concealed.

### Cleaner production and industrial ecology

The defensible contribution is procedural: source-side improvement cannot be
called cleaner production until the receiving pathway has been followed and
validated. The work complements rather than replaces LCA and industrial ecology.

### Environmental systems and LCA

Receiving impacts are gated, not quantified. No language should be restored that
equates low population burden, land classification, or closed-loop labeling with
environmental safety.

### Thermal energy

The measured source and finite dispatch are useful, but the modes remain
sensitivities because demand, capture, route, auxiliary power, storage design,
cost, lifecycle inventory, and authorization are incomplete.

### Optimization

The central result is an objective-specification diagnostic. The optimum value
can be reproducible while receiver identity is non-unique and environmentally
ineligible.

### Reproducibility and statistics

The frozen values, source manifests, deterministic build, reference registry,
claim matrix, unit separation, and validation checks are strengths. The scenario
ensemble is descriptive and must not acquire inferential language.
