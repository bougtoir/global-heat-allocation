# JCP final GO / WEAK GO / NO-GO decision

## Decision

**WEAK GO for submission after author-controlled actions.**

## Basis

The package passes the manuscript, reference, archive, traceability,
reproducibility, and visual checks. Its central contribution is appropriately
scoped as a conservation-aware, evidence-gated method for detecting thermal
burden shifting. The global branch is explicitly theoretical, the measured case
is auditable, incompatible units are not combined, and the practical endpoint
remains zero evidence-supported modes.

The decision is not full GO because author metadata and submission-time
confirmations remain unresolved. It is not NO-GO because the scientific claim no
longer depends on claiming a supported project endpoint, complete LCA, or safe
receiving sink.

## Author actions required before upload

1. Confirm author affiliation and full postal address.
2. Confirm the funding statement.
3. Confirm the competing-interest declaration.
4. Confirm any required institutional, ethics, or consent statement.
5. Confirm final approval by all authors and originality declarations.
6. Archive the final public repository release and insert its permanent DOI.
7. Repeat the live JCP guide-for-authors check immediately before submission.
8. Complete any submission-system fields, including reviewer requests if
   required.

## Scientific and editorial risk retained

- The global model is a marginal atmospheric-slab screening model, not a coupled
  climate, ecosystem, or project model.
- The practical branch has six conditional modes and zero supported endpoints.
- No case-specific lifecycle inventory or complete installed-cost model exists.
- A handling editor may regard burden shifting as established; novelty therefore
  rests on the operational conservation-and-evidence test and its auditable
  two-scale demonstration.

## Submission rule

Do not remove the evidence limits or replace missing inputs with nominal values
to obtain a stronger endpoint. Once the author actions are completed without
changing the scientific claims, the package can advance from WEAK GO to GO.
